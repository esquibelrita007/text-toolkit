Privacy Policy for Text Toolkit

Text Toolkit is a free online collection of browser-based tools for text editing, SEO optimization, formatting, writing, and developer utilities. This privacy policy explains how information may be handled when visitors use this website.

Information We Collect

Text Toolkit does not require users to create an account, log in, or submit personal information to use the tools. Most tools are designed to work directly in the browser.

Tool Input Data

Text, code, URLs, or other content entered into tools is processed in your browser whenever possible. Text Toolkit does not intentionally store, sell, or collect the content you paste into the tools.

Cookies and Analytics

Text Toolkit may use basic cookies or analytics tools in the future to understand website traffic, improve performance, monitor errors, and make the site more useful. Analytics data is generally used in aggregate and not to personally identify visitors.

Third-Party Services

Some pages may use third-party scripts, libraries, or services to provide tool functionality, improve performance, or support website features. These third-party services may have their own privacy practices.

External Links

Text Toolkit may include links to other websites. We are not responsible for the content, privacy policies, or practices of external websites.

Children's Privacy

Text Toolkit is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated as Text Toolkit grows, adds new tools, or changes how the website operates.
